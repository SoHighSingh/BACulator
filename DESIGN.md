# BACulator — design system: "Glass"

Chosen 18 Oct 2026 from five built directions (Result, Night dial, Forecast, Health card, Glass); the prototypes were removed on 23 Oct 2026 and stay in git history (before commit 9ece249). The register is Vercel / shadcn / Claude desktop: dark zinc, light caught on 1px edges, dense glass cards for anything you read, and lighter liquid glass for the chrome that floats over content. Premium comes from material, restraint and motion that answers the finger, never from loud fills or big buttons.

Two modes share one system:

- **Night screen (Operate):** a still field of blue light behind one number, translucent cards below it that let the field show through, and the glass chrome: the hazard key and You key at the top, and the dock. Content scrolls under the glass.
- **Landing (Persuade):** the same world, centred like a product page: a two-line headline, a white squircle key, and the real night screen floating in a glass frame on the light field, driven by try-it chips.

Retired: the pint-glass FluidGauge and every liquid effect, the paper docket and stamp, amber as the action colour, flat slab buttons, hairline-free "tone step only" rule (1px edges are now part of the material).

- **Cards (`.card`):** translucent, a denser cut of the dock's glass (user decision, 18 Oct 2026 late): `--card: rgb(15 15 18 / 0.68)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(150%)`, a 1px `edge` border, 14px corners; rows inside are split by 1px `edge` dividers. Over the brightest red the field reaches (≈ rgb 118 33 25) the card composites to ≈ rgb(48 21 20): `ink` 16.2:1, `ink-2` 6.6:1, `alert` 8.9:1, so everything on a card clears 4.5:1 and status words 7:1 whatever is behind. Under `prefers-reduced-transparency` and `prefers-contrast: more` they fall back to solid `surface` with no blur (and an `ink-2` edge for more contrast). Everything you read sits on a card or on the field, never on the lighter chrome glass alone.
- **White key:** the primary action is white (`ink`) with `bg` text: a full-width squircle key on the dock, a square squircle for compact keys, a soft blue under-glow. No other filled colour buttons.
- **Corners: one squircle language.** Every container is a squircle (`corner-shape: squircle`, set globally in `globals.css`; Chrome 139+), with a radius of about 25–30% of its height for controls, the proportion of the mark's tile (rx 16 on 64). Nothing is a pill and no key is a disc; "pill" in this document names the status element, not a shape. Tokens (`@theme`, so `rounded-key` etc.): `mark` 8px (≤ 32px marks: chart tooltip, live-value tag), `tile` 11px (40–44px: the brand tile, icon tiles in rows), `key` 13px (48–56px controls: keys, chips, icon keys, the hazard key, the composer), `card` 14px, `row` 16px (rows, inputs, panels inside sheets), `primary` 20px (64–72px white keys), `panel` 28px (dock, centred modals, the landing's glass frame), `sheet` 32px (bottom sheets, top corners). Where `corner-shape` is unsupported (Safari, Firefox) the same tokens step down to about 0.8× (key 11, primary 16, panel 23, sheet 26…) so plain arcs read with the same tightness. True circles exist only for marks that are not containers, via `rounded-circle` (`corner-shape: round`): the range bar's band, tick and peak ring, the chart's now-dot, size-option rings, the slider thumb and track, the sheet's grab handle. Concentric insets keep the family: the mic's live ring is key + 6px; the landing video sits 8px inside its 28px frame at 20px.

## Type (Geist, one family; Geist Mono never)
- Number: `clamp(88px, 27vw, 116px)`, weight 500, tracking −0.055em, line-height 0.88, pulled left 0.04em so the glyph's edge (not its box) sits on the gutter. The heading "Estimated blood alcohol" carries the estimate wording and "about" is spoken only (user decision, 18 Oct 2026 late; it stays in the range line and the scrub readout).
- **Third digit** (user decision, 22 Oct 2026, "so it feels more accurate"): the headline is the model rounded UP at the third place and drawn as two spans on one baseline: the big two places ("0.09") and the third digit small at its bottom right ("7"): 0.35em, weight 500, tracking −0.02em, `tabular-nums` so it never shifts width, `margin-left: 0.06em`, `ink` at 70% (tinted from the foreground, not grey: ≥ 9:1 on the blue field, ≈ 5.7:1 over the brightest red, and ≥ 31px). Both spans come from one rounded value (`displayPoint`), so the digits never contradict and never read lower than the model; this replaces the old two-place `ceil2` headline (0.0963 now draws 0.09|7, not 0.10). It changes in place every few minutes as `now` ticks; only the big digits play the 520ms blur-in, the small digit swaps with no motion. No small digit at 0.00 or "under 0.01". Screen readers are unchanged: the live line and the button's name keep the two-place "about 0.10" (`aboutPoint`); voice never reads a three-decimal figure. Only the hero has the third digit; the range, Numbers, the sheet and the scrub readout stay two places.
- Range line "Likely 0.07 to 0.14": 20px / 500. Section titles 18px / 600. Body and rows 18px / 400–500. Secondary 18px in `ink-2`. Nothing on the night screen under 18px except chart axis labels (16px).
- The add key: 20px / 500, sentence case, normal tracking ("Add a drink"). No oversized drink names.
- Landing headline `clamp(44px, 7.2vw, 92px)` / 600, tracking −0.055em, two lines at every width ("Blood alcohol, / with error bars.").
- Figures: proportional lining for the number and prose; `tabular-nums` only where figures stack (tab rows, axis).
- No eyebrows, no tracked uppercase labels.

## Time of a drink
Default now. Chips: Now / 15 min ago / 30 min ago / 1 hour ago, then **Earlier…** which opens a slider (5-minute steps, back to the start of the 48 h window) with −15 / +15 minute buttons either side and a live read-out "9:40 pm · 1 h 20 min ago". Dragging is never the only way. The same control edits a logged drink's time.

## Motion (all off under `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Nothing moves at rest, with two loops: the mic ring while the microphone is live, and the rising light's breath. **The rising light breathes** (user decision, 18 Oct 2026 late: "pulse slowly so it shows more of that motion"): while the estimate is rising, and only then, the comet's glow and core (`.momentum-rise`, night screen and hero strip) run `momentum-breathe`, a 4s ease-in-out loop, opacity 1 → 0.55 → 1 with a hair of lengthening from the tick toward the ring (`scaleX(0.96)` at the trough, origin at the tick, so it never passes the ring). The band, the tick, the ring, the label and the number hold still; nothing breathes while falling or at zero; under reduced motion the light sits still at full strength.
- Number change: 520ms blur-in (blur 10px → 0, rise 10px). Range band: 520ms width/position ease.
- Composer open: 280ms rise. Sheets: 340ms rise. Status line: 260ms slide-in. **Exits** (user, 22 Oct 2026: "they open really nicely, and then they close statically"): every surface leaves the way it came in about 70% of its open time, on `--ease-exit` (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`, the same decelerating family as `--ease-out` but less front-loaded, so a sheet leaving over its full height is seen to travel), transform and opacity only, and takes no taps while it leaves. Sheets 240ms `sheet-down` (down past the bottom edge, dimming to 0.6); scrims fade in 340ms and out 240ms with their sheet; centre dialogs (the big curve, Before you start) 280ms in / 200ms out as fade + scale 0.96 (`.dialog-card`, `.dialog-scrim`; tw-animate retired); the composer 200ms `rise-out`, then the keys rise back in (`usePresence`). A leaving surface keeps showing what it last showed (`useHeld` / `Held`), never an empty state. Sheets can be pulled down by the handle or header (past 110px, or a flick) and spring back 240ms if not; the Close key stays. Under reduced motion every exit is instant. Press: scale 0.975 in 160ms. Pointer light on dock and rows follows the finger (radial highlight), only while touched.
- Curve draws in once (900ms clip). Easing everywhere: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; the one exception is the field's colour bloom, `cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.25, 1)`, because the ease-out made 900ms read as a 300ms snap.
- **Blue → red (~900ms), when the shown number reaches 0.05:** 0–250ms the mark's band goes red (`.bm-tone` opacity) while its white tick slides 6 units right on the same clock (`.bm-tick` transform, 250ms `--ease-out`), the needle moving up. Underneath, the blue light leaves (700ms) as the red blooms (900ms), half in at ~450ms. **Red → blue:** the band goes blue and the tick slides back (250ms) as the red leaves. The colour and the tick only transition inside an animating `.night-status` (`.is-anim`); elsewhere the mark renders its final state. **The hazard key** lands beside the mark when the first drink is counted, scaling 0.6 → 1 from its left edge with opacity (`hazard-in`, 300ms, from 120ms); when the night clears it is simply gone. Only transform and opacity; the You key never moves. All of it plays only for a change the person made (a notice landed in the last 3s): on load, or when data arrives already red, the final state shows with nothing playing. Under reduced motion it is an instant swap. (Retired, 18 Oct 2026 late: the clip-path morph into the words pill.)
- Swift port: blur-in ≈ `.blurReplace` + `.spring(response: 0.5, dampingFraction: 0.9)`; glass = `.glassEffect()` / `.ultraThinMaterial`; sheets = system sheet.

## Targets and the impaired-user floors
Primary add 72px tall and full width; every target ≥ 48px; ≥ 8px between targets and ≥ 12px around the primary. Status words ≥ 7:1, everything else ≥ 4.5:1, control edges ≥ 3:1. Repeat drink = 2 taps (Add a drink, then a recent); a new drink ≤ 3 taps (type, size, Add); typing and voice are optional. Undo instead of confirm. The microphone is only requested when the mic key is tapped.
